Privacy

Route 66 is a Historic Route 66 travel companion published by Pruthvi Studios. Drive it either way: Chicago to Santa Monica, or Santa Monica to Chicago. The legal owner is PruthviRaj G. This policy covers the live app at route66drive.com (and www), plus the iPhone and Android shells that load it.

What we collect

Account. Email, display name, and the sign-in provider you pick (email, Google, or X). First signup becomes the owner; later accounts wait for approval.

Trip. Stops you save or mark reached. These stay on your account so the same trip follows you from phone to laptop.

Photos and comments. Pictures you upload at a stop, optional captions, ratings, and comments. You choose public or private when you upload.

Location. Used only after you tap Near me or Drive here, on that device, to sort nearby stops or hand a named Route 66 destination to Google Maps. We do not store a location history, do not track in the background, and do not sell location.

Camera and microphone. Used only on Director’s cut, which is owner-only, to film cue-card clips. Clips stay on the device unless the owner shares them.

What we do not do

No ads. No advertising identifier. No sale of personal data. No analytics SDK. Sign-in with Google or X is handled by those companies under their own policies.

Keep / delete

Trip rows and photos live in the app database while your account is open. Email PruthviRaj G via the public issue tracker or the in-app profile if you want an account or a photo removed.
